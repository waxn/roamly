# Android app: design, updates, navigation, screens

_Read when: Anything under mobile/ except tracking capture._

**Look (2026-11, "calm" pass).** Warm graphite ground (Night `#121416`, Day `#F2F0EC`) with a soft **sand** accent (`#D4B48C` Night / bronze `#8F6A40` Day) for the route, active state and links, muted categorical colours, and **coral reserved for Record** (`AtlasColors.record`/`onRecord`; it turns `danger` red while recording). Typeface is **Figtree** (variable font `res/font/figtree_variable.ttf`, `FigtreeFamily`, OFL); Plex Mono remains for `AtlasType.mono`. The `Atlas*` token names and the squircle/haptics rules below are unchanged; the orange/green-black wording below describes the earlier palette. The map is **dark by default** (see Map controls).

**Mobile design system (Night Atlas).** The app has its own design system, deliberately *not* a port of the web's Field Journal look (that port was tried and disliked). **Restyled 2026-11 away from the "generated" look** (blue-black ground, orange on every control, glowing glass, tracked mono caps): the ground is now a green-black (Night) / pale lichen (Day) survey-sheet palette with muted categorical colours, **primary buttons are ink (`text` on `bg`), and orange is reserved for Record, the track and active state**; radii are tighter (tag 8 / control 12 / panel 16 / dialog 24), shadows are small and neutral (no accent glow), the dock's Record button is flat, and section titles/eyebrows are sentence-case Plex Sans, not uppercase mono. It stays map-first with frosted panels over the map and big tabular numerals for the figures. Light mode ("Day Atlas") keeps the structure on a cool paper ground. **Dynamic (Material You) colour is deliberately not used.** `ui/theme/Theme.kt` defines `AtlasColors` (read through `Atlas.colors`: `bg`, `surface`/`surfaceHigh`/`surfaceHighest`, `glass`, `line`/`lineStrong`, `text`/`textMuted`/`textFaint`, `accent`/`onAccent`/`accentSoft`, categorical `cyan`/`lime`/`violet`/`rose`/`amber`/`sky` (`ramp` lists them), `success`/`warning`/`danger`) and maps them onto an M3 `ColorScheme` so stock M3 pieces (date/time pickers, sheets) match. `RoamlyTheme(darkTheme)` is the entry point; `UserPreferences.darkMode` null = follow the system (Settings → Theme: System / Night / Day; `clearDarkMode()` returns to System). **Never hardcode a hex in a screen — add it to `AtlasColors`.** Typography is IBM Plex Sans throughout (Fraunces was dropped; its TTFs are unused) with Plex Mono for `labelSmall` eyebrows/section titles and `AtlasType.mono`; `displayLarge`…`displaySmall` and `AtlasType.figure`/`figureSmall` use tabular numerals (`tnum`) so counting numbers don't jitter. Corners stay the web's squircle (`ui/theme/SquircleShape.kt`, `SquircleRadius.tag/control/panel/dialog`; `SquircleShape(50)` is a pill); **new shape ⇒ `SquircleShape`, never `RoundedCornerShape`.** The map's default basemap is `"Auto"`: OpenStreetMap tiles, tinted into a night map in dark mode by `basemapNightFilter` (an invert-and-cool `ColorMatrix` on the tiles overlay); `"Dark"` is that night map in either theme. Set a basemap with `MapView.applyBasemap(name, token, dark, loadingColor)`, never `setTileSource` alone, so the filter and the loading-tile colour come with it; every MapView in the app goes through it (`rememberAtlasMapView` uses `"Auto"`). **Don't use CARTO tiles in the app**: CARTO now needs a per-site API key (see `map.md`) that the app has no way to fetch, and stamps "API KEY REQUIRED" over every tile without one.

**The kit (`ui/kit/`) is the only place screens get controls from.** `Surfaces.kt`: `Panel` (tones `Raised`/`Sunken`/`Glass`/`Accent`/`Outline`, tappable with `onClick`/`onLongClick`), `IconTile`, `Tag`, `Dot` (optionally pulsing), `Skeleton` (shimmer). `Controls.kt`: `AtlasButton` (`ButtonKind.Primary/Secondary/Ghost/Danger/Glass`, `loading`, `compact`), `IconAction`, `AtlasChip`, `SegmentedSwitch` (gliding indicator), `AtlasSwitch`, `AtlasField`, `ChipRow`, `InfoPair`. `Layout.kt`: `ScreenHeader` (tab title with eyebrow), `TopBar`/`PushedScreen` (pushed routes), `SectionTitle`, `ListRow` (its `onClick` is deliberately the **last** parameter, after the `trailing` slot, so `ListRow(...) { open() }` is a click — with `trailing` last that lambda ran during composition and navigated on its own), `ToggleRow`/`RowGroup`/`RowDivider`, `StatBlock`, `EmptyState`, `ErrorNote`, `Foldout`, `AtlasSheet` (two overloads: the plain one, and an `@ExperimentalMaterial3Api` one taking a `SheetState` so a sheet can animate away before acting), `AtlasDialog` (`destructive`; `onDismissAction` when the dismiss button must differ from tapping outside). `Notice.kt` is the transient message pill. `Art.kt`: `RouteArt(seed)` (generated contour+route art for things with no picture, e.g. trip cards) and `TrackSketch` (real track points over **OSM tiles** painted statically by `kit/StaticBasemap.kt` — memory + OkHttp disk cache, night-tinted in dark mode, tiles drawn 1:1 or sharper, plain background offline — **use it, not a live MapView, inside scrolling lists**: a MapView in a `LazyColumn` is disposed/recreated per scroll and steals taps). `MiniMap.kt`: `configureOsmdroid`, `rememberAtlasMapView(interactive)` (theme-following basemap, osmdroid's zoom buttons hidden), `MapViewHost`, `addGlowLine`, `fitTo`, `TrackMap`, `avatarMarker` (round lettered marker drawable) and `MEMBER_TRACK_COLORS` (web-matched per-member palette). `Motion.kt`: `AtlasMotion.snappy/glide/gentle` springs and `Modifier.tappable` — **the one way to make something tappable**: ripple, springy press shrink and a haptic (`Feel.None/Tick/Select/Click/Confirm/Heavy`).

**Haptics (`ui/kit/Haptics.kt`).** One vocabulary for the whole app via `rememberHaptics()`: `tick` (detents, steppers), `select` (tabs, chips, segments), `click` (buttons/rows), `toggle(on)`, `confirm`, `reject`, `longPress`, `gesture`, plus two patterned effects, `success` (scan matched, signed in, saved) and `thud` (recording/tracking started or stopped). Each maps to the richest `HapticFeedbackConstants` value the API level has (these honour the system touch-feedback switch and need no permission); `success`/`thud` use `VibrationEffect.Composition` primitives where the vibrator supports them — hence the `VIBRATE` permission — and fall back to a view constant. The in-app switch is Settings → Haptic feedback (`UserPreferences.hapticsEnabled`, default on), mirrored into `HapticSettings.enabled` by `MainActivity` and `SettingsViewModel.setHapticsEnabled`.

**Mobile branding:** Adaptive launcher icon — the orange Roamly mark (`drawable/roamly_favicon.png`, the same trail-blaze mark as the website favicon) on black (`drawable/ic_launcher_background.xml`). `android:windowBackground` is black to kill the cold-start flash. `ui/SplashScreen.kt` draws topographic contour rings outward from the centre while a glowing route arcs in from a corner; the mark lands with a spring and a haptic click, the "ROAMLY" wordmark rises, and it fades into the app. The contour wobble and route are reseeded from `System.nanoTime()` on every launch. `RoamlyNavHost` plays it until it finishes and `isLoggedIn` has resolved. `LoginScreen` sits on slowly drifting contours.

**Mobile in-app updates.** The app is sideloaded (no Play Store), so it self-updates. CI (`.github/workflows/mobile-release.yml`) publishes a signed APK (`Roamly<version>.apk`) as a GitHub Release asset on each `mobile-v*` tag. Two **server** endpoints proxy that so the app only ever talks to its own configured server: `GET /api/mobile/version/` (`views.mobile_version_check`) fetches `releases/latest` from GitHub (repo = `settings.MOBILE_UPDATE_REPO`, default `waxn/roamly`, env-overridable), parses the `mobile-v*` tag → `version_name` + release notes + the `.apk` asset, and returns it — cached 15m in Django cache (`mobile_latest_release`) to stay under GitHub's 60 req/hr unauth limit (there's no invalidation on publish, so a new release is otherwise invisible until the entry expires; `?refresh=1` forces a fresh GitHub fetch, rate-limited by a 2m cooldown lock so the public bypass can't burn the GitHub quota); `GET /api/mobile/apk/` (`views.mobile_download_apk`) streams the APK, fetching it from GitHub into `MEDIA_ROOT/apk/` once and serving from disk thereafter (`media/` is gitignored + a Docker volume). Both are public (no auth). On the **mobile** side: `RoamlyApi.getLatestVersion()`/`downloadApk()` (`@Streaming`), `data/repository/UpdateRepository.kt` (semver-compares installed `versionName` vs server, downloads to `cacheDir/updates/`, installs via `FileProvider` + `ACTION_VIEW` package-installer intent — needs the new `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` permission and routes to `ACTION_MANAGE_UNKNOWN_APP_SOURCES` if not yet granted), `ui/update/UpdateViewModel.kt` + `UpdateBanner.kt`. `RoamlyNavHost` runs a throttled (~24h, `UserPreferences.lastUpdateCheck`) on-launch check and shows a dismissible "Update available" banner; Settings → About has a manual "Check for updates" button (shared VM instance). Android can't silently install for a non-system app — the OS install-confirmation screen always shows. **Server change ⇒ build; mobile change ⇒ rebuild APK.**


**Mobile caching:** `data/cache/DiskCache.kt` — Gson-backed JSON file cache (`cacheDir/api_cache`) persisting last successful response per screen for cold-start / offline. Wired into Stats, Adventures, Pals, Journals VMs. Wiped on sign-out. `StatsCache` is the fast warm-cache layer; `DiskCache` is the cold-start fallback beneath it.

**Mobile navigation shell.** Four root pages behind a **floating bar** (`ui/FloatingNav.kt`, a frosted squircle pill with a gliding indicator): **Map · Write · ● · Activity · You**. The tabs are `Tab` entries (Trips is labelled **Write**, with the edit-note icon, since it holds Adventures and Journals); the **round coral Record button is not a tab but the centre slot** (`RecordSlot`, drawn when `onRecord` is given, so not in Simple Mode). Tapping it calls `RecordLaunch.request()` and goes to the Activity page, whose `RecordScreen` opens the kind sheet; while recording it is red and pulsing and just opens the live screen (`Tab` enum: routes `map`, `trips`, `record`, `you`). Ask/Search is no longer a tab: it is a pushed route (`Routes.ASK`) opened from the map's search pill, and its `ScreenHeader` draws a back button through `LocalScreenBack` (set by `RoamlyNavHost` only for `backHeaderRoutes`). The bar shows only on the four roots, and hides while an activity is being recorded so the live screen has the whole display; a coral dot marks Activity while recording, and the centre button pulses red. Tab content pads its bottom by `LocalDockPadding` (`dockAwareBottom()`/`screenPadding()`): bar height + margin + nav bar on roots, just the nav bar on pushed routes; the map draws edge-to-edge and every other root gets a status-bar scrim. Root ↔ root is a quick crossfade (`TAB_MS` 150); a pushed route slides in over the full width (`PUSH_MS` 220, `PushEase`, no fade) while the page beneath drifts a fifth of the width; back reverses it, so the predictive-back drag carries the page straight off. **Simple Mode** shows only Map + You and hides Record and the search pill. **Trips** (`TripsTabScreen`) holds Adventures | Journals (headed "Journals") behind a `SegmentedSwitch` plus a live `FeaturedBlogCard`; **Ask** (`SearchTabScreen`) holds Ask | Search; **Activity** (`ui/record/RecordScreen.kt`) is described below (the "Record an activity" bar opens a kind sheet, and picking a kind starts recording); **You** (`ui/you/YouScreen.kt`) is a **grid of panels**: the tracking hero, a notifications-off notice when the OS blocks them, then two-column `HubTile`s for Stats, Flights, Health, Location data, Family Circle, Tracker, Update (when available), Settings and Diagnostics, and sign out behind a confirm. Tracking on/off lives in `settings/TrackingControl.kt`'s `TrackingHeroCard`, which owns the whole safe-start flow. `MainActivity` re-runs `enableEdgeToEdge` with light/dark `SystemBarStyle` whenever the theme changes.

**Activity page (`ui/record/`).** Idle: a sideways row of kind tiles (Ride/Run/Walk/Hike/Row/Other) on top, your recent activities as a scrollable **two-column grid** of cards like the web's (a `TrackSketch` mini-map drawn from the list API's encoded `thumb` polylines via `decodePolyline`, then title, date and "km · moving time"), with recording started from the dock's centre button (there is no separate Start bar). The last list is held process-wide (`RecordViewModel.lastRecent`) so a revisit paints instantly, and refreshes on every resume. Tap a card → `ActivityDetailScreen` (route `activity/{id}`: a map of the smoothed track from `GET /api/activities/<id>/track/`, distance, moving time, avg/max speed, climb, notes, delete); long-press deletes. Recording swaps the page for the live map, figures and Stop; **it is back-stack scoped** because `RecordViewModel` watches the tracking DB only while a session is live.

**Health page (`ui/health/`).** `HealthDataSection` reads what the server already holds, like the web page: a Steps/Distance/Calories switch, 7 days/30 days/90 days/1 year chips, totals, a tappable bar chart from `GET /api/health/daily/` (`buckets`, server-chosen granularity; a day-sized bar opens that day) and a day detail from `GET /api/health/day/<date>/` (totals, hourly bars, workouts). Sync, permissions and workout import sit under "Sync from this phone".

**Notifications.** Three channels (`RoamlyApp.createNotificationChannels`): tracking (low), Family alerts (high), daily note (default). Reliability work: the evening reminder uses an **exact** Doze-piercing alarm when the OS allows it (falling back to `setAndAllowWhileIdle`), re-armed on every fire, app start and boot; **`PushRegistrar`** registers the *current* FCM token on every sign-in/app start (`onNewToken` alone fires before first login on a fresh install and was never retried, so Family alerts silently never arrived); `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is requested once after the first sign-in; and You shows a notice while notifications are blocked.

**Tab ViewModels are Activity-scoped, and that is what makes a tab switch instant.** Each tab's ViewModel is resolved in `RoamlyNavHost` against **`activityOwner`** (the `LocalViewModelStoreOwner` captured *outside* the `NavHost`, where it still resolves to the Activity) and passed into the screen, rather than letting the screen take `hiltViewModel()`'s default. The default owner is the destination's own `NavBackStackEntry`, and the tab navigation pops with **`saveState = true`, which clears that entry's `ViewModelStore`** — so every switch destroyed the tab's ViewModel and the next visit re-ran its `init`: a **synchronous Gson read off disk on the main thread** (`DiskCache.get`) plus a fresh round of network calls, behind a spinner. The same treatment covers Map, Trips, Journals, Stats (a pushed route now, still activity-scoped), the trip blog and Flights.

Two consequences are load-bearing and must be kept in step:

- **The owner is passed down, not the ViewModel.** `hiltViewModel()` constructs on the spot, and these `init` blocks fire network calls immediately — resolving them at the top of `RoamlyNavHost` would run them during the splash, against the placeholder base URL before login resolves, which is the exact trap `MapViewModel.init`'s own comment describes (it waits on `serverUrl.first { !it.isNullOrBlank() }`; the others do not). Handing the *owner* to each destination keeps construction lazy — first visit to that tab — while keeping the scope wide.
- **`SettingsViewModel` is deliberately excluded and must stay excluded** — it backs the You tab and the Settings/Diagnostics routes, each back-stack scoped. It is the only one of these with *continuous* collectors, and two of them run against exactly the infrastructure tracking depends on — a Room `Flow` on the **tracking database** (`pointDao().unsyncedCountFlow()`, re-queried on every fix the service inserts) and `WorkManager.getWorkInfosByTagFlow` — on top of eighteen file-backed DataStore flows. Activity-scoped, those ran for as long as the app was alive, **including a whole drive with the screen off** (backgrounding does not destroy the Activity), in the **same process** as `LocationTrackingService` (the manifest declares no `android:process`), contending on the same SQLite database `savePoint()` writes into. That starved the capture loop — fix gaps stretched from a configured 10s toward the 45s `MIN_ALARM_FLOOR_MS` × `CATCHUP_MULTIPLIER` backstop, which is what a "tracking got slow and unstable" report looks like. **Before hoisting any ViewModel here, check its `init` for a collector that outlives the screen; a one-shot load is safe, a `Flow` on `TrackingDatabase` or WorkManager is not.**
- **A surviving ViewModel no longer refreshes by being recreated**, so each of those destinations carries a `LaunchedEffect(Unit)` calling the VM's own loader (`loadTrips()` / `refresh()` / `load()`). The composable is still recreated per visit, so that is once per visit, and each loader only raises a spinner when it has nothing cached to show — the switch stays instant and the data still catches up. **A new tab added here needs the same two lines, or it will show whatever it loaded the first time, forever.**

**Sign-out therefore recreates the Activity** (`onLoggedOut` → `Activity.recreate()`, falling back to the old `navigate(Login) { popUpTo(0) }` if the Activity can't be found). Routing to Login alone would leave the previous account's trips, journals and stats sitting in an Activity-scoped store for whoever signs in next, with nothing left to trigger a reload. Recreation drops that store wholesale and lands on Login by itself, since `logout()` has already cleared prefs; `splashDone` is `rememberSaveable`, so the splash does not replay. **`StatsCache` is `@Singleton` and outlives even that**, so `logout()` calls `statsCache.clear()` explicitly alongside `disk.clearAll()`/`store.clear()` — it was previously wiped by nothing at all, and leaked the prior account's figures across a sign-out.

**Map overlays cull in geographic space before projecting.** `HeatmapOverlay.draw` and `PointsOverlay.draw` run on **every** pan/zoom frame over the whole accumulated point set — `MapViewModel`'s `accumulator` keeps merging viewport detail (`BBOX_LOAD_LIMIT` 8k a time, on top of a `PERIOD_LOAD_LIMIT` 20k overview) and never prunes, so after some panning that list is tens of thousands of rows. Both loops used to project **every** point — allocating a throwaway `GeoPoint` per point per frame — and only then discard the off-screen ones, so the per-frame cost scaled with everything ever loaded and the GC churn is what made the map drag.

`ViewportCull` reads `mapView.boundingBox` once per draw, pads it by the sprite/dot radius converted to degrees (`pixelsToDegrees`), and rejects with two double comparisons before any projection happens; survivors are projected through a **single reused `GeoPoint`** (`setCoords`) and reused `Paint`/`Rect`/`RectF`/`Point` fields. Per-frame cost now scales with what is on screen. Two details matter: `contains` returns **`true` when the viewport could not be read**, so a bad frame falls back to drawing everything rather than to a blank map; and when the viewport straddles the antimeridian (`lonWest > lonEast`) the visible span is the **outside** of `[minLng, maxLng]`, hence the `or` rather than an `and`.

**The map camera auto-fit is keyed on the data, not on the composable.** `didAutoFit` lives on `MapViewModel` (reset in `setTimePeriod`/`setCustomDateRange`, the two places the dataset changes) rather than in a `MapScreen` `remember`, which dies with the composable — so every switch away from the Map tab and back re-ran the whole fit, both paying a full main-thread pass over the accumulated points and yanking the camera back from wherever the user had panned to. The bbox is also computed as a single min/max loop instead of materialising a `GeoPoint` per point to hand to `BoundingBox.fromGeoPoints` (note the constructor is `BoundingBox(north, east, south, west)`).


**Mobile local-first location store:** `data/local/LocationStore.kt` backs a Room table (`synced_locations`, keyed by server location id) in its own `MapCacheDatabase` (see **The capture loop is isolated from the UI** — it is rebuildable, so it is kept out of `TrackingDatabase`). Sync is incremental via the locations API cursor (`sort_dir=asc` + `before_value`/`before_id`; `has_more`/`next_before_value`/`next_before_id`). `sync()` runs on foreground and `MapViewModel` init; `MapViewModel` observes `store.state` (a `StateFlow<SyncState>`) and reloads on sync complete. Map paints from store: decimated overview (`id % stride`) + full-resolution viewport dots from local DB on pan/zoom. "Have I been here?" scans the entire local history. `SyncedLocationDao.inTimeRange(startMs, endMs)` supports custom date ranges. Wiped on sign-out.

**Mobile feature parity:** Map (map-options sheet: time periods incl. a custom date range via Material3 `DateRangePicker` + basemap selector; ‹ › range stepping and the "what I did today" pill + sheet, see `quick-notes.md`; refresh; heatmap; GPS dot tap → point detail dialog naming the recording device; "have I been here?"), Adventures (see **Mobile Adventures parity** below), Stats (monthly drilldown: tapping a month bar expands a daily Canvas chart with grid lines; tapping a day navigates map to that date), Journals (day track is a real osmdroid `MapView` with a glowing accent line, in a full-height editor sheet; tap card opens map for that date), Settings (with **Diagnostics** page: coverage bar chart from `/api/diagnostics/location/` pre-filled with device_id, CSV share via FileProvider; ; Location data is a pushed route from You — see below), **Search** (in the Ask tab, beside Ask; place results expand inline to show each day, tap a day → map date filter). **The legacy Pals feature (removed on the web in migration `0059`) is fully deleted from mobile** — `ui/pals/*`, `PalRepository`, the Groups Pals tab and all `api/pals/*` declarations are gone; the shared `PalMember` model was kept (it types `TripResponse.members`). `MapViewModel.navigateToDate(dateStr)` sets a single-day custom range and clears `focus` so MapScreen auto-fits the viewport; called from search, stats drilldown, journal track taps, and Ask date links. `setTimePeriod`/`setCustomDateRange` also clear `focus = null` so auto-fit always fires on new data. `MapViewModel` scoped to Activity (created outside `NavHost.composable` blocks in `RoamlyNavHost`) so Journal and Stats can trigger map navigation. The UI uses the Night Atlas theme and the floating dock (see **Mobile design system** and **Mobile navigation shell** above). Power-user/admin features remain web-only.

**Mobile Adventures parity (`ui/trips/`).** The mobile trip detail page (`TripDetailScreen.kt` + `TripDetailViewModel.kt`, backed by the same `GET /api/trips/<id>/` `_trip_detail_inner` response the web uses) now reads and writes most of what the web Adventure has. **Read:** the **Story** block document renders read-only via `StoryBlockRenderer.kt` (`StoryBlock` dispatches on `block.type` — heading/paragraph/divider/callout/photo_grid/location_card/timeline; `map_embed` is scoped down to a tap-to-open-full-map card rather than a live embed; unknown block types render nothing; `content` is read as a loose Gson `JsonObject` mirroring the web's untyped renderer; `[^pin:ID]` refs become inline numbered badges). **Day Log** and **Itinerary** render as sections (`TripSections.kt`), and blurbs show star rating + category badge + a photo/video thumbnail row. The trip map (mini + `TripFullMapScreen`) overlays each member's own track in the web-matched `MEMBER_TRACK_COLORS` palette (owner = index 0) with a swatch legend, plus the itinerary as numbered `Marker`s joined by a dashed `Polyline`. A shared `MediaThumbnailRow` (with a full-screen lightbox; videos open externally) is reused by story photo grids, blurbs, and day notes. **Write:** members create/edit their own **Day Log** entries (title/body/linked-place dropdown/up-to-10 photos or videos via `PickMultipleVisualMedia`), create/edit/reorder **Itinerary** stops (form + move-up/down buttons that swap `order` server-side; transport options match the web plan page), post richer **blurbs** (title/star-rating/category/photos, multipart), and creators get an **Invite** action (mint/show the `/adventure/join/<token>/` link with copy + native share + reset) plus a member list with per-member remove. New models (`BodyBlock`/`PlacePayload`/`MediaItem`/`PlannedStop`/`DayNote`/…) extend `TripResponse`; new `TripRepository`/`RoamlyApi` methods wrap the already-existing day-note/plan/invite/member endpoints. `trip_create_blurb` was made `@csrf_exempt` (matching its siblings) and taught to accept `rating`/`category` so a session-authed mobile multipart post works. **Story block *editing* is deliberately not on mobile** (the web block editor is a much larger effort; mobile gets full read parity without it). **Mobile change ⇒ rebuild APK.**

**Gson ignores Kotlin defaults on most response models.** Gson only runs a Kotlin class's default values when every constructor parameter has one (that gives it a no-arg constructor). Otherwise it allocates the object without calling a constructor, so a field missing from the JSON comes back **null even when declared non-null with `= emptyList()`**. `TripResponse` is like this (`id`, `name`, `start_time` have no default): the list endpoint (`trips_api`) sends no `locations`, so `TripResponse.locations` is declared `List<TripLatLng>?`. Before this, the Adventures cards' `pointCount` threw a NullPointerException and the Trips tab crashed on open. A field that only one endpoint sends must be nullable, or read defensively, on any model shared between endpoints.

**Data screens + boarding-pass scanning (`ui/stats/`, `ui/flights/`, `ui/health/`, `flights/`).** There is no Data tab any more: Stats, Flights, Health and Location data are pushed routes opened from tiles on the You tab (each takes `onBack`). Health and Location data stay back-stack scoped (Health's ViewModel is not a one-shot load); Stats is activity-scoped.

**Flights in the app** (`FlightsScreen`: Log | Map | Stats, mirroring the web; the log is boarding-pass-style ticket cards; map lines are osmdroid `Polyline`s with `isGeodesic = true`; distance follows the phone's region since the app has no unit setting) and **scanning**: `flights/Bcbp.kt` parses the IATA **BCBP** barcode (Resolution 792: fixed-width mandatory fields per leg, then optional conditional blocks addressed by 2-hex-digit sizes — read only as far as they exist, never throwing; the flight date is a **day of year with no year**, resolved to the match nearest today within −330/+35 days). JVM tests in `app/src/test/.../BcbpTest.kt` build passes field by field to the spec's widths; `mobile-check.yml` runs `testDebugUnitTest`. `flights/PkPass.kt` reads `pass.json`'s barcode text out of an Apple Wallet pass. `flights/PassDecoder.kt` decodes images with **ML Kit's bundled barcode model** (`com.google.mlkit:barcode-scanning`, the model in the APK) rather than the Play-Services variant, so scanning works on de-Googled phones — the reason `LocationSource` exists; only PDF417/Aztec/QR/Data Matrix are enabled. `ScanPassScreen` (route `flights/scan?uri=`) runs CameraX (`LifecycleCameraController` + `MlKitAnalyzer`) behind an animated viewfinder, with torch, a screenshot picker and a wallet-pass picker; the preview frame becomes the pass photo, and a multi-leg pass asks which flight. `FlightEditScreen` (route `flights/edit`) confirms and corrects everything; after a scan it runs the AeroDataBox lookup automatically when the server has it (`lookup_enabled` on `GET /api/flights/`), picking the leg that matches the pass's route and **never overwriting** what the pass said. The draft crosses the two routes on an Activity-scoped `FlightsViewModel` (no collectors). **Sharing** an image, or sharing/opening a `.pkpass`, into Roamly (`MainActivity` intent-filters → `flights/IncomingShare`, offered on a fresh launch or `onNewIntent` only, so a rotation can't reopen it) opens the scanner on it once signed in. `CAMERA` is requested at runtime and declared `required=false` — without a camera the pickers still work. **Mobile change ⇒ rebuild APK + tag; deploy the server first** (the app needs the 409 + `lookup_enabled` + pass endpoints).

**Mobile Location Data screen (`ui/settings/LocationTableScreen.kt`).** The mobile mirror of the web `/data/` page — a server-backed, sortable, filterable, paginated list of raw GPS points with per-row delete, opened from the You tab. Reuses `GET /api/locations/` read as the flat `locations` array (new `getLocationsTable` call + `LocationTableRow`/`LocationTableResponse`, leaving the map-sync `LocationsResponse` path untouched), with the same keyset-cursor pagination (`before_value`/`before_id` → `next_before_value`/`next_before_id`) and `sortBy` direction rules as the web table (default desc for timestamp/speed/battery, asc otherwise; the server's echoed `sort_key`/`sort_dir` are trusted over local intent). Card-per-row layout with range/device/sort pills that open option sheets; delete hits `DELETE /api/locations/<id>/delete/`. **Mobile change ⇒ rebuild APK.**

**Mobile map controls (`MapScreen.kt`).** The top is one row — a **search pill** (opens Ask/Search), **refresh** and **preferences** — then the **range pill** (‹ label ›; tap opens `RangeSheet`, the period chips plus a custom date range) beside the "what I did today" pill. Bottom: "Been here?" at the right, above the floating bar (Record lives in the dock). The range pill and the notes pill **share the top row's full width** (each `weight(1f)`; the range pill alone fills it when notes are hidden). The floating bar spans the screen with the same 14dp side inset (`DOCK_SIDE`) as the map controls, slots are equal-width. The launcher icon has a `<monochrome>` layer (the mark's alpha) so Niagara/Android 13+ can recolour it. The notes pill is 46dp tall like the other top-row controls. The old stats card is gone. The **basemap is dark by default** (`Dark`, an inverted-and-cooled OSM tile layer in either theme; `Streets`/`Satellite` are the alternatives; a stored `Auto` is read as `Dark`), and track dots are pre-rendered flat sprites per speed band (saturated blue/green/amber/orange/red, a thin dark rim, no halo) so a frame is only blits. The **heat glow shows only below zoom 9** (`HEAT_MAX_ZOOM`, fading over the last level); dots start at `DOT_MIN_ZOOM` 9 and are **thinned in screen space** (one dot per cell, ≤6000 drawn) so a spot you sit at stays readable. Detail loads from zoom 10 (`DETAIL_ZOOM`) and the pan accumulator is capped at 60k points (`MAX_ACCUMULATED`; past it, points outside the view are dropped). Eviction only drops points that arrived as viewport detail (`detailIds`), never the period overview. The basemap pref key is `map_basemap_v2` so everyone returned to Dark once. All layers and display choices live in one place, `MapOptionsSheet` ("Map preferences"), a scrolling `ModalBottomSheet` grouped like the web sidebar: three folding `OptionsGroup`s whose heads show `n on`: **Map display** (basemap chips, **fog of war**), **Track** (**transport** chips Walk/Cycle/Vehicle/Plane/Still/Unclassified, **filled gaps**, **hide outliers**, **hide track**) and **Extra data** (**my places**). Toggles keep the sheet open. They live in `data/prefs/MapLayers.kt` (one DataStore string, `map_layers`, tolerant `encode`/`decode`; defaults as the web: filled gaps on, outliers hidden, group open/closed state included) and reach the map via `MapViewModel.updateLayers`. The track filters are client-side, `MapUiState.visibleLocations()`, which needs `LocationPoint.flag` and `transportMode`: the locations API always sent them, so they were added to `LocationPoint` and to the `SyncedLocation` cache (`MapCacheDatabase` v2, destructive, so the first open after upgrading re-syncs; v3 is a real 2→3 migration that only swaps `(lat, lng)` for the covering `(lat, lng, timestampMs)` index, keeping the cache). **Caveat:** incremental sync only fetches new points, so a point re-flagged or re-classified later on the server keeps its old value here until the cache is rebuilt. The other layers are osmdroid overlays in `MapLayerOverlays.kt`: `FogOverlay` (`GET /api/fog/`, whole history, a dimmed `saveLayer` with a soft 200 m hole per cell punched `DST_OUT`, cells bucketed by whole degree; drawn under the track), `InferredOverlay` (`GET /api/inferred/` for the range, a dashed line per edit batch, refetched when the range changes) and `PlacesOverlay` (`GET /api/places/`, each place's radius ring in its colour with its name from zoom 12). Refresh refetches places and filled gaps. Not ported: globe, scratch map, snapped view, replay and the cell layers. **Refresh** (`MapViewModel.refresh`) forces a full `LocationStore.sync()` — not the throttled `syncIfDue()` — then clears the pan-accumulated detail dots and repaints period + stats, holding its inline spinner until the repaint lands (hence `loadDataNow`, the awaitable body split out of `loadData`). The **point-detail dialog names the recording device**: the locations API nests points under their device rather than repeating it per point, so `LocationPoint.deviceId` is a non-Gson field filled in by `LocationStore.toPoint()` (the Room row already stored it — no migration) and by `MapViewModel.flatten()` on the server-fallback path; `MapUiState.deviceNames` maps it to a display name from `GET /api/devices/` (now typed as `DevicesResponse` rather than a raw `ResponseBody`), falling back to the raw id. "Have I been here?" is a squircle `Surface`, not a pill, and is answered by `LocationStore.haveIBeenHere`: per-day counts and closest point aggregated **in SQL** over a 200 m box (`SyncedLocationDao.nearDays`, so a home you sit at all day returns hundreds of rows), dwell time computed exactly only for the newest 30 days (`nearTimes`; older days show no duration), and the area name from one `LIMIT 1` label query, answered by the covering index alone; the answer is cached in `MapViewModel` for 10 min within ~45 m and **prewarmed** from the last-known fix when the map opens, so the button usually answers instantly — it used to load the whole ~11 km neighbourhood (hundreds of thousands of rows at home) and parse every timestamp string; the old path remains only as the empty-store fallback. **Raw data** (You → Raw data, `LocationTableScreen`) has the web /data/ source selector: recorded points (sortable, deletable) plus every source from `GET /api/data/sources/` (generated, trash, hidden, cells, health, recaps, year-ago), each rendered generically from the `columns` the server sends (`GET /api/data/source/<key>/`, keyset paged). Health charts (`HealthData.kt`) can be dragged across: the readout follows the finger bar by bar and lifting opens that day. **Arrows:** the time pill's ‹ › and its dropdown arrow, and the group heads, all draw one chevron (`Icons.Rounded.ChevronRight`, rotated, 20dp — `Chevron`/`CHEVRON_SIZE`), as the web panel does; the zoom/record buttons (`ZoomFab`) and the "Loading detail…" chip carry the same outlined plate as the other map controls.

**Mobile "Ask" tab (Ask + search).** The third dock slot is **always present** — search must work without an AI provider. `ui/search/SearchTabScreen.kt` hosts it: with AI Ask configured it shows a `SegmentedSwitch` **Ask** / **Search** (Ask first); without, the switch is hidden and the tab is search only. `onAsk = askEnabled && page == 0` decides — there is deliberately no effect forcing `page` to Search when `askEnabled` is false, because it *is* false on the first frame (before the cached flag loads) and would strand AI users on Search. `com.roamly.ui.ask.AskScreen`/`AskViewModel` mirror the web AI Ask feature (`ask_api`). `GET /api/profile/ai-config/` returns `{ai_ask_enabled, configured}`; `AskViewModel` caches `configured` in `UserPreferences.askEnabled`. Chat is `POST /api/ask/` — `AskRepository.sendMessage` sends the full history + `tz_offset` (JS `getTimezoneOffset()` convention). `AskScreen` shows suggestion chips when empty, the user's turns as accent bubbles and replies beside a sparkle tile, with a floating composer sitting above the dock or the keyboard (`max(LocalDockPadding, ime)`). It renders the reply's markdown-lite (`**bold**` + `[label](/path)` links) with `LinkAnnotation.Clickable`; only `/map/?date=YYYY-MM-DD` links navigate (`MapViewModel.navigateToDate`). **Mobile change ⇒ rebuild APK.**
**Map previews (Adventures, Journal).** `GET /api/trips/` sends each trip a `preview` (~60 `[lat, lng]` pairs, id-stride sampled in the DB and cached per trip/end/count by `views._trip_preview`) and `GET /api/journals/` sends `track` for the newest 40 photo-less entries (`views._journal_day_preview`, cached 1h for today/yesterday, 12h older; `?tz=` = browser minutes offset). The previews are ~200 points (trips) / ~150 (journal day), the sample size being part of the cache key. The Adventures card, the Journal row and the Activity cards draw them with `TrackSketch` (basemap tiles, safe in lists); the web feed draws the same points as an inline SVG (`trackSnapshot` in `journals.html`) when an entry has no photo.

**Activity detail (`ActivityDetailScreen.kt`).** Map + figure tiles (distance, moving, avg/max speed, climb, elapsed, descent, points) and, from the track endpoint's `chart` series (`ActivityTrackResponse.chart`, even-in-time, flattened across segments), a **Speed** and an **Elevation** chart you can drag a finger along (readout = value and time into the activity). `MapView.fitTo` now waits for the first layout when the view has no size yet (a map swapped in after its data arrives used to frame a 0×0 box and stay at world zoom, which read as "the map doesn't load").

**Stats page (`StatsScreen.kt` + `StatsCharts.kt`).** Beyond the hero and the by-month bars it now carries the web's distance block: tiles (km travelled, active days, km per active day, best day), a **Distance travelled** bar chart (bars sum several days past ~120; drag to read). **A page-wide range picker (7d / 30d / 90d / 1y / All, default All) sits under the lifetime hero and drives everything below it**: a range summary (`GET /api/stats/?start_date&end_date`), the distance block (the all-time series cut client-side; the year heatmap and the calendar-year Periods/by-month bars only for 1y and All), the transport split (`/api/transport/breakdown/` with dates) and the Countries/Cities lists (`/api/visits/` now also takes `start_date`/`end_date`), the **Daily distance** year heatmap (Monday-first columns, year ‹ ›, tap/drag a cell for its day) and **How you travelled** (per-mode km and hours). Data: `GET /api/distance/?all=1` (`DistanceResponse`) and `GET /api/transport/breakdown/?all=1` (`TransportResponse`), both served from the stats snapshot server-side; `StatsViewModel` loads them independently of the rest.
