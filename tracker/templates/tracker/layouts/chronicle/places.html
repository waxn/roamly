{% extends "tracker/base.html" %} {% block title %}Places - Roamly{% endblock %} {% comment %} Chronicle's Places (v2): a grid of place tiles (small basemap thumbnail, name, points, last seen) grouped by how recently you were there. Opening a tile fills the page right of the sidebar with a large map and a collapsible side panel (stats, visit history, cities, notes, notifications). The open place is part of history (?place=ID, pushState), so the browser Back button closes it. Speed: the list's point_count / last_seen come from the nightly snapshot, but /api/places// is a LIVE scan of every point inside the geofence (not precomputed), which is what made big places (home, work, anywhere you were today) slow to open. The panel therefore paints from list data at once, shows a cached detail instantly (sessionStorage, revalidated in the background), joins any in-flight prefetch, and prefetches on hover/focus/touch and for the first few tiles at idle. The point stream waits for the stats so the two scans do not compete. Basemap: the user's chosen basemap (localStorage roamly_map_tile_style, list from #roamly-basemaps when the view supplies it, else the three built-ins). Thumbnails are rendered one or two at a time off-screen, exported as JPEG and cached, so there is never a grid of live GL contexts.
