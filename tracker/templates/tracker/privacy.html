privacy policy

last updated: february 2026

1. overview

roamly is a self-hosted, open-source location tracking application. this privacy policy explains how your data is handled when using a roamly instance.

2. data collection

roamly collects only the data you explicitly provide:

  • account information: username and password (hashed) for authentication.
  • location data: gps coordinates, timestamps, altitude, speed, and battery level sent from your tracking device.
  • trip data: trip names, dates, and associated places you create.

3. data storage

  • all data is stored on the server where this roamly instance is hosted.
  • no data is sent to roamly developers or any third party by the application itself.
  • the instance operator is responsible for securing the server and database.

4. third-party services

roamly may use the following external services:

  • map tiles: map imagery is loaded from tile providers (e.g., openstreetmap). your browser sends requests to these providers, which may log ip addresses.
  • reverse geocoding: when enabled, coordinates are sent to openstreetmap's nominatim service to resolve place names.

5. data control

you have full control over your data:

  • export all your data at any time in csv or gpx format.
  • delete individual location records or your entire account.
  • the application does not prevent you from accessing or removing your data.

6. cookies

roamly uses session cookies for authentication only. no tracking cookies or analytics are used by the application.

7. self-hosted instances

since roamly is self-hosted, the privacy practices of each instance depend on its operator. this policy covers the application's default behavior. instance operators may have additional policies.

8. changes to this policy

this policy may be updated as the application evolves. changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

9. contact

for privacy concerns, please open an issue on the github repository or contact the operator of this instance.
